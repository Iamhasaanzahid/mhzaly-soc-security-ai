How the platform proves itself.

A finding only reaches a report when it survived the precision gate. Each reported finding carries a deterministic evidence fingerprint and commits into a hash-chained ledger — tampering with any past finding breaks the chain, loudly.

01 · Score & gate

0–100 confidence per candidate, floor 70, severity floor MEDIUM, de-duplication across scans.

02 · Verify

Real evidence only — calibrated probes, TLS chain checks, content signatures.

03 · Chain

Append-only hash chain per scan run, head hash + proof pack export.

04 · Posture

Explainable 0–100 posture with per-factor drivers and scan-to-scan delta.
